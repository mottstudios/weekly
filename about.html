Antisocial Media

What is this?

Seven weekly prompts. They're all simple: take a photo, go for a walk, call someone, read a poem ... stuff like that. Stuff you probably already do.

Do I have to do all seven

No. Nothing counts anything. If you don't want to do one of the seven prompts, you can swap it. If there are others you don't want to do, ignore them. "Roll your own" and write them down somewhere.

How do I do them

Just do them. Or not. That's it.

What's the catch

There isn't one.

What's it for

For fun.

When do I need to do them

Do them at your leisure. If you check back on a Monday, a new set will be available. It's up to you to remember to check. The previous weeks will remain available, so if you want to go back and do or re-do earlier prompts, you can.

Did you make this

This was made by Tom Mott. Which Tom Mott? Who cares. Meet them all!

Privacy and data info

There are no accounts, analytics, cookies, or server. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere because there is nowhere for it to go.

The ones you check off are written into your own browser’s storage on whatever device you're using. Nobody else sees it.

Because your dots live in your the browser, they don't follow you. A different browser, device, or clearing your site data will clear them.

Tip

Bookmark the link or save it as a shortcut.

One of the links is dead

If that happens, make up something else to do instead.
